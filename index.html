How it works
  • Arrows point from a service to what it depends on. A service is degraded when anything it depends on is down, directly or further along.
  • Uptime counts only the three services users hit: web, api and checkout.
  • You are one engineer. A fix takes about a second, postgres takes two, and you can work on one thing at a time.
  • Tapping a service that isn't down costs you a probe and finds nothing.
  • MTTR is the mean time from a fault appearing to its fix.
  • The daily shift is the same for everyone and can be played once a day. Practice shifts are random.
