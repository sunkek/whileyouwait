    How it works
    • Arrows point from a service to what it depends on. A service fails if it has a fault, or if anything it depends on is failing.
    • Monitored services alert when they fail. Grey ones have no monitoring, so you can't see their state.
    • There are one, two or three root causes, and you aren't told how many. Tap a service to act on it.
    • Check logs takes 2 minutes and tells you whether the service has its own errors, is failing because of a dependency, or is clean.
    • Fix takes 5 minutes (postgres failover takes 8) and repairs the service if it has a fault. Otherwise the time is lost.
    • Every minute, each failing user-facing service (web, api, checkout) adds a user-minute of downtime. Only actions move the clock, so take as long as you like to think.
    • Par is what the best possible strategy scores on this incident, playing without knowing the answer and treating one, two and three root causes as equally likely. A lucky guess can beat it.
