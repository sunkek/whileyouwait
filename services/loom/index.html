Reset restores the start; taps still count. Showing wrong cells marks the result as assisted.

How to play
  • Weave the target. Coloured squares are a vertical thread showing on top; cream squares are a horizontal pass showing on top.
  • Each of the 16 vertical threads hangs on one of 4 frames: its square in the threading above the cloth. Tap a column there to move the thread to the next frame.
  • Each of the 16 passes presses one of 4 treadles: its square in the treadling on the right. Tap a row there to move the pass to the next treadle.
  • The tie-up in the corner says which frames each treadle lifts: a dark square joins a treadle (its column) to a frame (its row).
  • Where a pass lifts a thread's frame, the thread shows. Threads whose target columns look the same belong on the same frame.
  • Par is the taps that undo the day's scramble. A tap only steps forward, so there is no undo.
