How to play
  • Lay a path of fallen blocks from flag to flag, side by side or end to end. A sage block must not fall. Par is the fewest pushes that can do it.
  • Tap an amber block, then an arrow, or swipe on it. A block falls as many squares as it has dots.
  • It knocks over the first standing block it reaches, and that block falls the same way.
  • The edge or a fallen block stops it, and the line ends there.
  • Gap counts the squares your path still needs. A path can't cross a sage block.
  • Undo takes a push back, and the push still counts.
